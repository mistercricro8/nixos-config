Writing rules:
- Do not use bold (except headings), italics, nor monospaced fonts (unless in blocks of code separate from the paragraphs)
- Do not use parentheses for explanations, abbreviations must only be expanded the first time they are used in a document.
- Do not talk about internal tooling, the prompt or anything unrelated to the topic requested by the guide.
